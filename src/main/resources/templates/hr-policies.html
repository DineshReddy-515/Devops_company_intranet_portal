Remote Work Policy Updated
Last revised: Jun 28, 2026 · Owner: People Ops
▸
Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and team coverage needs.
  • Requests are submitted through the Time & Attendance system at least 3 business days in advance.
  • Core collaboration hours (10 AM–3 PM local time) apply regardless of location.
  • Company equipment must remain on the approved VPN while working remotely.
Leave & Time Off
Last revised: Jan 5, 2026 · Owner: HR
▸
All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off annually, plus company-observed holidays.
  • PTO requests go through your manager with at least 5 business days' notice where possible.
  • Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.
  • Sick leave is tracked separately and does not draw from the PTO balance.
Code of Conduct
Last revised: Nov 12, 2025 · Owner: Legal & Compliance
▸
Sets expectations for professional behavior, conflicts of interest, and workplace respect across all Meridian offices.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind.
  • Employees must disclose outside business interests that could create a conflict.
  • Violations may be reported confidentially through the Ethics Hotline.
Benefits Enrollment
Last revised: Oct 1, 2025 · Owner: Total Rewards
▸
Health, dental, vision, and retirement plan options available to all employees after a 30-day waiting period.
  • Open enrollment runs annually in July.
  • Qualifying life events allow enrollment changes outside the open window.
  • 401(k) contributions are matched up to 4% of base salary.
Expense Reimbursement
Last revised: Aug 20, 2025 · Owner: Finance
▸
Business expenses must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the transaction date.
  • Travel, client meals, and approved software subscriptions are reimbursable.
  • Manager approval is required for expenses over $500.
Parental Leave
Last revised: Jul 3, 2025 · Owner: HR
▸
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks, for births, adoptions, and fostering.
  • Leave can be taken continuously or split within the first 12 months.
  • A phased return-to-work option is available on request.
IT & Acceptable Use
Last revised: Mar 15, 2025 · Owner: IT Security
▸
Governs appropriate use of company devices, networks, and accounts.
  • Company devices are for business use; incidental personal use is permitted within reason.
  • All laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint protection enabled.
  • Report lost or stolen devices to IT Security immediately.
Performance Reviews
Last revised: Jan 10, 2025 · Owner: People Ops
▸
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice yearly, in January and July.
  • Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and goal setting for the next cycle.
  • Compensation adjustments are considered during the July cycle.
